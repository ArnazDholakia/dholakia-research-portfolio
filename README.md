# Arnaz Dholakia — Research Portfolio

Welcome to my research portfolio!

I am a **climate economist and climate scientist** working at the intersection of **climate extremes, socioeconomic impacts, and quantitative risk assessment**. My research focuses on understanding how physical climate hazards translate into economic and social outcomes, and how differences in indicators, exposure, and adaptive capacity influence assessments of vulnerability.

## Research Interests

* Climate extremes and socioeconomic impacts
* Climate–economy linkages
* Vulnerability and impact assessment
* Hazard indicators and indicator sensitivity
* Uncertainty in climate-risk assessment
* Economic and financial capacity to adapt
* Temporary and persistent climate shocks
* Labour mobility and climate-induced migration
* Agricultural and environmental impacts of climate change

## Research Approach

My work combines **climate data, socioeconomic datasets, statistical modelling, and geospatial analysis**. I am particularly interested in connecting hazard-side information with economic models and understanding how methodological choices affect conclusions about climate risk.

Methods and tools I have worked with include:

* **Python:** pandas, NumPy, xarray, SciPy, statsmodels
* **R:** extreme-value analysis, distributions, copulas, trend analysis
* **STATA, MATLAB, NetLogo**
* **ERA5, CMIP6, ISIMIP and FADN datasets**
* **NetCDF and geospatial data processing**
* Panel regression and econometric analysis
* Extreme-value theory and return-period analysis
* Copula and dependence modelling
* Mann–Kendall and Sen's slope
* QGIS, GeoPandas, Rasterio and Cartopy

## Selected Research Themes

**Climate extremes & agriculture**
Assessment of how heat extremes and compound climate conditions affect agricultural production and how indicator definitions influence inferred vulnerability.

**Macroeconomic climate shocks**
Analysis of temporary and persistent climate-driven shocks across sectors and regions using outputs from integrated macroeconomic modelling frameworks.

**Climate-induced migration**
Statistical perspectives on migration and non-migration, considering environmental exposure alongside socioeconomic conditions and capacity to adapt or relocate.

**Environmental and agricultural emissions**
Quantification and spatial analysis of livestock enteric methane emissions across Indian states.

## About Me

I completed an **MSc in Integrated Climate System Sciences (Economics & Social Sciences)** from Universität Hamburg and a **BA in Economics with a minor in Applied Mathematics** and **Post Graduate Diploma in Interdisciplinary Studies and Research** from FLAME University.

My broader goal is to develop **quantitatively rigorous approaches to understanding climate risk and its socioeconomic consequences**, with particular interest in how uncertainty, heterogeneity, and adaptive capacity shape impact assessments.
